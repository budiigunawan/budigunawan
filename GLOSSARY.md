# Budi Gunawan — Personal Site

The public record of Budi Indra Gunawan as a frontend engineer moving toward fullstack: what he has shipped, where he has worked, and what he has written.

## Language

**Project**:
A discrete piece of software Budi built or shipped, shown on the site as the primary evidence of his ability. Every Project is either personal or professional.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, case study, work sample

**Work Entry**:
One employment relationship — company, role, and date range — recording the responsibilities Budi held there. Work Entries form a timeline and do not own Projects.
_Avoid_: work experience, job, position, career entry

**Stack**:
The technologies a Project was built with, shown on the project card.
_Avoid_: tags, tech stack

**Post**:
A published piece of Budi's writing on the site, technical or personal.
_Avoid_: article, blog entry, essay

**Retro**:
A yearly retrospective Post reviewing one year of career and life. A Retro is a kind of Post, not a separate content type.

**Contact Message**:
A message a visitor submits through the Contact form, readable only by Budi. It is the only thing on the site written at runtime.
_Avoid_: inquiry, email, submission

**Resume**:
The canonical PDF record of Budi's history, aimed at recruiters. It is a file, not a page: edited in Google Drive, published on the site as a copy.
_Avoid_: CV, resume page
