This site is a personal record, not a product. Visitors can send me a message through the Contact form. This page explains what that Contact Message contains, why it is stored, and who can read it.

## What a Contact Message contains

- Your name.
- Your email address.
- The text of your message.

The form asks for nothing else.

## Why it is stored

A Contact Message is stored so I can read it and reply to you. It is written to the database before the notification email is sent, so a message is not lost if email delivery fails. Storing it also lets the site limit repeated submissions from the same client; that check uses a hashed form of the sender's IP address, never the raw address.

## Who can read it

Only I can read Contact Messages. They live in a private, hosted database, and a copy is emailed to me through my email provider. Those services process the data on my behalf; I do not publish, share or sell it, and no one else reads it.
